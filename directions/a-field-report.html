§1

Same problem, different scales

Every system is agents at different scales trying to agree: teams, software, markets, bodies. I work on where they don't.

I've spent my career on the same problem at very different sizes: a pre-launch B2B platform, a small shop, systems moving millions of transactions a day. The tooling changes completely. The work doesn't.

Off the clock I read outside the usual product shelf (Sapolsky, James C. Scott, Donella Meadows, Michael Levin) because failure modes rhyme across organisms, institutions and release processes. Lately I write about where the story and the numbers part ways.

Most disagreements are definitional, and most bad systems are unexamined assumptions with a database attached.
