About

The work is always the same problem

I've spent my career on the same problem at wildly different scales — a pre-launch B2B platform, a mom-and-pop shop, systems moving millions of transactions a day. The tooling changes completely. The work doesn't: find where the process is fighting the people, and fix it.

I joined TireHub, the Goodyear/Bridgestone joint venture, a month before launch, and spent six years taking its eCommerce platform from nothing to best-in-class — payments, ERP sync, manufacturer data pipelines, a CRM I ended up owning outright. When the reporting didn't exist, I learned Crystal Reports, Power BI, and Tableau and built it.

Today I'm a Product Owner at Jack Henry, translating "this could work" into "this runs reliably in production" — and co-leading the AI Bridge Collective, helping teams of very different technical backgrounds get productive fast.

"Most disagreements are definitional, and most bad systems are unexamined assumptions with a database attached."

Off the clock: reading outside my lane (Sapolsky, James C. Scott, Donella Meadows — failure modes rhyme across organisms, institutions, and release processes), writing, playing music, and shipping the occasional side project — most recently an Obsidian theme, Report Writer.
