Writing Hype or Reality, the first Dynamic Perception essay: what a $250,000 place in line actually buys.

The work is always the same problem

A pre-launch B2B platform, a small shop, systems moving millions of transactions a day. The tooling changes completely; the work doesn't. When the reporting didn't exist, I learned the tools and built it.
